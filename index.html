Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

🎤

Voice Sync

Scroll speed follows your voice in real time — speak and it moves, pause and it stops. No word matching, just your natural pace.

⚡

Zero Install

One HTML file. Open it in Chrome or Safari and you're done. No app store, no Electron, no dependencies.

🖥️

Fullscreen Mode

Clean black fullscreen display with soft edge fades, a centre crosshair for camera alignment, and a hidden cursor.

🪞

Mirror Mode

Flip text horizontally for use with a physical teleprompter mirror or beam splitter rig.

🎛️

Live Controls

Adjust speed and text position on the fly with the overlay controls — no need to stop and restart.

💾

Auto-Save

Your script is automatically saved to localStorage so it's always there when you come back.

How It Works

Up and running
in seconds.

1

Open the file

Download the repo and open teleprompter.html in Chrome or Safari. That's it — no build step, no server.

2

Paste your script

Drop your text into the editor. Paragraphs are automatically separated with a diamond marker in the prompter view.

3

Set your preferences

Dial in your reading speed, font size, and text row position. Enable Voice Sync and mirror mode if you need them.

4

Hit Start and present

The prompter goes fullscreen. Use your voice, keyboard, or the mouse overlay to control playback.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Keep your hands free.

Space Pause / Resume
↑ / ↓ Increase / decrease speed
← / → Scrub backward / forward
ESC Exit to editor
